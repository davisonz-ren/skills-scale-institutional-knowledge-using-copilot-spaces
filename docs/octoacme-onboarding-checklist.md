# OctoAcme — Team Member Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist ensures new team members are effectively onboarded to OctoAcme projects with the right access, context, and support to contribute successfully.

## Onboarding Principles

- **Structured but flexible**: Follow the checklist but adapt to individual needs
- **Role-specific guidance**: Customize based on the team member's role
- **Progressive exposure**: Start with essentials, then layer in depth
- **Buddy system**: Assign an onboarding buddy for questions and support
- **Feedback loop**: Gather feedback to improve the onboarding process

---

## Week 1: Getting Started

### Pre-Day 1 (Manager/Lead)
- [ ] Send welcome email with start date, time, and logistics
- [ ] Provision necessary accounts (GitHub, Slack, email, project tools)
- [ ] Add to relevant Slack channels and email distribution lists
- [ ] Assign an onboarding buddy from the same or related role
- [ ] Share this onboarding checklist and relevant documentation links

### Day 1: Orientation
- [ ] Meet with manager for welcome and overview
- [ ] Review OctoAcme's mission, values, and team structure
- [ ] Complete IT setup (laptop, accounts, VPN, 2FA)
- [ ] Introduction to onboarding buddy
- [ ] Review project management documentation:
  - [ ] [README — Project Management Docs](README.md)
  - [ ] [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md)
  - [ ] [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)
- [ ] Join team standup or intro meeting

### Days 2-5: Role-Specific Deep Dive
- [ ] Review your role definition in [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)
- [ ] Meet key team members and stakeholders (schedule 1:1s)
- [ ] Review active projects and current sprint board
- [ ] Access and explore key repositories and documentation
- [ ] Set up local development environment (for technical roles)
- [ ] Review team communication norms and meeting cadence
- [ ] Shadow relevant meetings (standups, planning, reviews)

---

## Week 2: Role-Specific Onboarding

### For Developers
- [ ] Clone relevant repositories and run local builds
- [ ] Review coding standards and PR workflow documentation
- [ ] Set up local testing and debugging environment
- [ ] Complete a "starter task" or small bug fix
- [ ] Submit first pull request with buddy review
- [ ] Join code review sessions to observe practices
- [ ] Review CI/CD pipeline and deployment process

### For Product Managers
- [ ] Review product roadmap and backlog
- [ ] Meet with customers or review recent customer feedback
- [ ] Understand success metrics and how they're measured
- [ ] Review competitive landscape and market positioning
- [ ] Shadow customer calls or user research sessions
- [ ] Review past sprint demos and release notes
- [ ] Attend backlog refinement session

### For Project Managers
- [ ] Review current project charters and timelines
- [ ] Access project tracking tools (GitHub Projects, dashboards)
- [ ] Review risk register and dependency tracking
- [ ] Understand escalation paths and stakeholder map
- [ ] Shadow or co-facilitate a standup or planning meeting
- [ ] Review retrospective notes and improvement actions
- [ ] Understand reporting cadence and format

### For UX Designers
- [ ] Access design tools (Figma, Sketch, design system)
- [ ] Review design system and component library
- [ ] Understand user research repository and insights
- [ ] Review recent usability testing and findings
- [ ] Meet with developers to understand design handoff process
- [ ] Shadow a design review or critique session
- [ ] Review accessibility standards and guidelines

### For QA Leads
- [ ] Review test strategy and quality metrics
- [ ] Access test management tools and repositories
- [ ] Understand automated and manual testing processes
- [ ] Review defect tracking workflow and triage process
- [ ] Set up test environments and access
- [ ] Shadow testing activities in current sprint
- [ ] Review Definition of Done and acceptance criteria standards

### For Scrum Masters
- [ ] Understand team's sprint rhythm and ceremonies
- [ ] Review team velocity and burndown charts
- [ ] Access sprint board and backlog management tools
- [ ] Review retrospective action items and improvements
- [ ] Attend current sprint ceremonies as observer
- [ ] Meet with other Scrum Masters to share practices
- [ ] Review impediment escalation process

### For Business Analysts
- [ ] Review business requirements documentation standards
- [ ] Access stakeholder map and communication plan
- [ ] Review recent BRDs and functional specifications
- [ ] Meet with key business stakeholders
- [ ] Understand requirements traceability approach
- [ ] Shadow requirements gathering session
- [ ] Review process flow documentation tools and standards

### For Customer Success Managers
- [ ] Access customer relationship management (CRM) system
- [ ] Review customer health scores and success metrics
- [ ] Shadow customer calls and business reviews
- [ ] Review customer feedback and feature request backlog
- [ ] Understand escalation and support ticket process
- [ ] Meet with current customers (if possible)
- [ ] Review customer onboarding and training materials

---

## Week 3-4: Contribution and Integration

### Building Confidence
- [ ] Take ownership of a small deliverable or task
- [ ] Participate actively in team ceremonies
- [ ] Contribute to discussions and ask questions
- [ ] Start building relationships across the organization
- [ ] Share initial observations and questions with buddy

### Understanding the Broader Context
- [ ] Review organizational OKRs and how your team contributes
- [ ] Understand adjacent teams and key integration points
- [ ] Learn about upcoming initiatives and roadmap priorities
- [ ] Attend cross-functional meetings relevant to your role
- [ ] Review historical context (past releases, major decisions)

### Feedback and Adjustment
- [ ] 30-day check-in with manager
- [ ] Provide feedback on onboarding experience
- [ ] Identify any gaps in knowledge or access
- [ ] Discuss initial goals and expectations
- [ ] Update personal development plan

---

## 60-Day Milestone

- [ ] Deliver a meaningful contribution independently
- [ ] Demonstrate understanding of team processes and norms
- [ ] Build working relationships with key stakeholders
- [ ] Identify areas for process improvement
- [ ] 60-day performance and onboarding review with manager

---

## 90-Day Milestone

- [ ] Full contribution as an independent team member
- [ ] Mentor or support newer team members
- [ ] Contribute to retrospectives and process improvements
- [ ] 90-day review and goal setting with manager
- [ ] Celebrate successful onboarding!

## For Onboarding Buddies

### Your Role
As an onboarding buddy, you help the new team member feel welcome and supported. You're not responsible for everything—just a friendly guide.

### Best Practices
- Schedule regular check-ins (at least 2-3 times in the first week)
- Be available for questions, no matter how small
- Introduce them to other team members informally
- Share the "unwritten rules" and team culture
- Provide context on why things are done a certain way
- Give honest, supportive feedback
- Check in after their first few contributions

### Time Commitment
- Week 1: ~3-5 hours (includes meetings and ad-hoc questions)
- Week 2-4: ~1-2 hours per week
- Month 2-3: Occasional check-ins as needed

## Feedback and Continuous Improvement

This onboarding checklist should evolve based on feedback. After each onboarding:

1. Gather feedback from the new team member
2. Identify what worked well and what could improve
3. Update this checklist with improvements
4. Share learnings in retrospectives
