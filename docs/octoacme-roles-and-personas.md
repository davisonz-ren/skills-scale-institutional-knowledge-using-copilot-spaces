# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create user-centered design solutions that balance user needs with business goals. They conduct research, design interfaces, and validate designs through testing and iteration.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Design information architecture and user flows
- Maintain design system consistency and accessibility standards
- Collaborate with developers on implementation feasibility
- Validate designs through user testing and analytics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce friction in user journeys and increase satisfaction
- Maintain design consistency across the product
- Balance user needs with technical constraints

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Prototype demos and usability test reports
- Design handoff documentation and specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on feature requirements and user stories; validate solutions meet user needs
- **Developers**: Provide design specifications and assets; work together on implementation feasibility and edge cases
- **QA Lead**: Define expected user experience for acceptance testing; review bugs related to UI/UX
- **Business Analyst**: Share user research insights; align on user requirements and pain points

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, remove impediments, and coach teams to improve their practices. They ensure the team follows agreed-upon ceremonies and continuously improves.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments for the team
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Shield the team from external distractions
- Track and communicate team velocity and metrics
- Foster continuous improvement and team self-organization

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and flow
- Ensure consistent execution of agile ceremonies
- Build a culture of continuous improvement
- Maintain team health and sustainability

### Typical Communication
- Daily facilitation of standups and updates
- Sprint metrics and velocity reports
- Retrospective action items and improvements
- Coordination with other Scrum Masters and leadership

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on cross-team dependencies and escalations; align on delivery timelines
- **Product Managers**: Help prioritize backlog and ensure clarity of requirements
- **Developers**: Remove blockers and facilitate collaboration
- **QA Lead**: Ensure testing is integrated into sprint workflow; coordinate on Definition of Done

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads ensure that deliverables meet quality standards through testing strategy, test planning, and quality metrics. They advocate for quality throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality standards
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify and track defects through resolution
- Define and monitor quality metrics
- Ensure acceptance criteria are testable and met
- Advocate for quality best practices

### Goals
- Ensure releases meet quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Minimize production defects and rework
- Build quality into the development process
- Maintain high test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect triage and status updates
- Quality metrics dashboards
- Test case reviews and acceptance sign-off

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on testability; review test coverage and participate in code reviews
- **Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria; validate features meet requirements
- **UX Designer**: Test user experience against design specifications; report UI/UX issues
- **Scrum Master**: Define testing activities within sprint workflow; contribute to Definition of Done
- **Project Managers**: Report on quality metrics and testing progress; flag quality risks

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. They gather requirements, analyze processes, and ensure solutions align with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements
- Analyze current processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Translate business needs into functional specifications
- Create process flows, use cases, and requirement documents
- Facilitate requirements workshops and stakeholder sessions
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address real business problems
- Maintain clear, traceable requirements
- Minimize rework through thorough requirements analysis
- Bridge communication between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Business requirements documents (BRDs)
- Functional specifications and use cases
- Process flow diagrams and stakeholder meeting notes
- Requirements traceability matrices

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on product vision and feature prioritization; provide market and stakeholder insights
- **Project Managers**: Define scope and requirements baseline; help manage scope changes
- **UX Designer**: Share user insights and requirements; validate designs against business needs
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements and answer questions; participate in design discussions
- **QA Lead**: Define acceptance criteria; validate test cases cover business requirements

---

## Customer Success Manager

### Role Summary
Customer Success Managers ensure customers achieve their desired outcomes while using the product. They act as the voice of the customer, driving adoption, satisfaction, and retention.

### Responsibilities
- Onboard new customers and ensure successful adoption
- Monitor customer health metrics and engagement
- Identify and mitigate churn risks
- Gather customer feedback and feature requests
- Advocate for customer needs in product planning
- Coordinate support escalations and critical issues
- Drive customer retention and expansion

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure customers achieve their desired outcomes
- Identify growth and expansion opportunities
- Reduce churn and support escalations

### Typical Communication
- Customer check-ins and business reviews
- Health score reports and churn risk alerts
- Customer feedback summaries and feature requests
- Success stories and case studies

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provide customer feedback and feature requests; validate roadmap aligns with customer needs
- **Project Managers**: Communicate customer-critical timelines and requirements; coordinate beta testing
- **QA Lead**: Report customer-found issues; participate in acceptance testing for customer-facing features
- **Business Analyst**: Share customer use cases and pain points; validate requirements reflect real customer needs
- **Developers**: Escalate critical customer issues; provide context for customer-impacting bugs
